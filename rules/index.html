Invisalign deliveries (iTap board)

Added 11/20/2026 at Greenie's request.

  • A shipment is marked Delivered only when UPS tracking says Delivered. "Out for delivery" or an expected date is not delivered.
  • Every delivered card shows the full UPS delivery info: day, date, time, where it was left, and who received it. Example: Thu 11/19 10:02 AM, left at front desk, received by Tatianna.
  • This applies to all delivered Invisalign cards.
